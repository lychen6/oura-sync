Personal Oura Sync

This is a personal application for synchronizing my Oura health and activity data with my private Google Sheets fitness tracker.

Privacy Policy

This application is intended for personal use only. Health and activity data are accessed with the account owner's authorization and stored in a private Google Sheets document.

Data are not sold or shared for advertising. Access can be revoked by the account owner.
